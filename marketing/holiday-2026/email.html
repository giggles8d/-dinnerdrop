Turkey and Cranberry Fried Rice. About 15 minutes with 2 to 3 cups of chopped leftover turkey, rice, stir-fry vegetables and dried cranberries.

Cheesy Turkey Potato Soup (40 min). Stir in shredded turkey once the potatoes are tender.

Tortellini Alfredo with Ham and Peas (20 min). Diced leftover ham, one pot.

White Bean and Ham Soup (40 min). Six bowls, and it freezes well.

Chicken and Stuffing Casserole (45 min). Use turkey for the chicken and your leftover stuffing for the boxed mix.

Our recipe pages list fresh turkey or chicken. Cooked leftovers work in each one. Reheat them to 165°F.

One rule from the USDA: cooked turkey, ham and stuffing keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge. For food cooked Thursday, that means finish it by Monday, Nov 30. Freeze the rest now.
