# DinnerDrop Puppies (new series, Nov 14 2026)

Format: real-looking puppies (photoreal video, no talking mouths) with the puppy's inner-voice narration, like the "what my dog is thinking" trend. Humans appear only as hands, legs and backs, so no face consistency problems. Real app screens in the middle of every episode. 18-22 s, 9:16.

Cast
- Biscuit: golden retriever puppy, about 4 months, red collar. Narrator. Dramatic, food-obsessed, thinks every dinner is about him.
- Pickle: black-and-tan miniature dachshund puppy, green collar. Biscuit's skeptical little sister. Silent, reacts with head tilts and side-eye.

Voice: warm, playful, slightly deadpan American voice for Biscuit, no accent.
Tools: start frames (nano_banana) from one cast reference; motion clips on Kling 2.5 Turbo Unlimited (free); voiceover on Higgsfield TTS (about 1 credit); app screens rendered from real recordings in a frameless card.
Rules: puppies never eat human food on screen; no on-screen AI label, disclosure in caption; no fake testimonials.

Episode ideas
1. "5 PM panic" (Ep 1): humans stare into the fridge every night; Mom gets DinnerDrop; now dinner just happens.
2. "Grocery bag inspector": Biscuit inspects the Instacart delivery and is disappointed there is nothing for him.
3. "Family vote": everyone texts their dinner vote; Biscuit votes chicken every single time.
4. "The lost list": Mom forgot the list at home; Biscuit is not surprised; now it's on her phone.
5. "Taco night": Pickle hears the word taco and loses her mind.
