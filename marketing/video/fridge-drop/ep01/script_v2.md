# Frank & Drop — Ep 1 "Mustard" (v2, ~21 s, vertical) — after ChatGPT + Gemini review

Frank: tired, deadpan white fridge, eyes on the door, always shown from a 3/4 angle. Dry, slow voice.
Drop: brown paper grocery bag, fixed folded corner, big DinnerDrop orange-dot badge facing camera, little arms. Organized, helpful, teases Frank (never Mom).
Mom: hands, silhouette, never the joke. Real app screens. Live kitchen under the end overlay (no static end card) so it loops.

S1 (0-2 s) The door is already being yanked open. Frank, 3/4 angle, one slow defeated blink. Text: "5:03 PM. No dinner plan."
  Frank: "Again? Still mustard."
S2 (2-5 s) Inside view: mustard up front; three half-used salsa jars, string cheese, half an onion behind it. Mom's hand pauses, then pulls back.
  Frank: "Lots of food. Zero dinners."
S3 (5-7 s) Mom sets her phone on the counter. Drop lands next to it with one controlled hop and taps the screen.
  Drop: "Let's plan next week, big guy."
S4 (7-14 s) Real app screens, close crops: cook-time / no-mushrooms preferences, 5 dinner cards, the list, tap "Send to Instacart".
  Drop: "Five dinners around her family. One list. Send to Instacart."
  Frank (off-screen): "Even the one who hates green things?"
  Drop: "Even him."
S5 (14-18 s) Text: "After checkout and delivery." Mom's hands load groceries into Frank. Drop straightens one jar with unnecessary precision. Mustard still visible.
  Frank: "Finally. Mustard isn't the main course."
S6 (18-21 s) Logo + official App Store badge + "Free to start" over the live kitchen; Drop pats Frank's door.
  Drop: "DinnerDrop. Start free on the App Store."  (hard cut on "Store" for the loop)

Reviews of v1: ChatGPT concept 8.5/10, script 7/10 ("approve the concept, revise Ep 1"). Gemini concept 6.5/10 (Frank alone 8.5), script 6/10.
