## What changed from V2 and why
1. **Cut the cooking-question scene (gym bleachers).** Both reviewers said cut it: it's a fourth feature, a new location and 6 s that dilute the main promise. It's saved for Ep 2.
2. **No silent app screens.** Both flagged the two 5 s silent stretches as the biggest drop-off risk. Now her voice runs over every app screen, and each app section opens with ~1 s of her face. (Builder change: the clip's audio plays under the app screens.)
3. **Voting is explained.** Gemini said "1 4 5" means nothing to a cold viewer. Now an outgoing "Pick 3 dinners: reply 1–5" bubble comes first, and the voiceover says what's happening.
4. **Cart moved earlier,** from 23 s to about 14 s (ChatGPT).
5. **Spoken CTA.** Both scored the CTA lowest. She now says the offer and the code out loud, and the "I finally sat down" ending that sounded like the video was over is gone.
6. **Clearer skit framing.** Both raised the AI-testimonial risk. It's now labeled an AI skit on screen in the first seconds, as well as on the end card and in the caption. The personal result lines ("I told it once… every week", "first time I've sat down since 5 a.m.") are cut, and the lines describe what the app does.
7. **Hook.** Kept the "fourteen people alive" line. Gemini called it the strongest asset; ChatGPT offered a safer "harder shift" version. It's the character's job, not a claim about the app, so it stays. The bridge "So I stopped deciding. Watch." (called generic) is now "So they vote now.", which leads straight into the voting demo. She's already looking at the phone at frame 1, and the bubbles come in one at a time so the first 2 s aren't crowded.
8. **End card.** Dropped "Things your meal planner can't do, Ep 1" (both: vague, could read as a contradiction). It now reads "Try it free, no card".
9. **Caption.** Code and link moved above the "…more" fold (Gemini), #singlemom swapped for #workingmom, and a curiosity-style YouTube title (Gemini).
10. **Not taken:** ChatGPT's "show one retailer only". The app does hand off to all three and it's an allowed claim, so the voiceover names all three while the screen shows the Instacart hand-off.
