## Fixes the review should make (Changed / Overclaimed)

1. **Ollie**: replace "pivoted to a general $25/mo personal assistant" with "now a general text assistant (free, $25 or $100/mo); meal planning with Instacart/Walmart/Amazon ordering is still one feature".
2. **eMeals price**: there is no monthly plan. Say "$59.99/yr (about $4.99/mo) or $37.50 per 3 months". Don't claim "card required" (not on a public page). Say the trial renews into a paid plan unless cancelled.
3. **Plan to Eat**: it does send lists to grocery services ("Deliver Groceries"), so don't call it "list only". The yearly plan does not auto-renew by default. A $24.50/yr sale banner is counting down.
4. **Samsung Food**: don't name Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh or Kroger as connected retailers; no Samsung Food page names them.
5. **HelloFresh**: label per-serving and shipping prices as third-party (NerdWallet Jul 2026; shipping about $11, varies). Everything else is from HelloFresh's own home page and terms.
